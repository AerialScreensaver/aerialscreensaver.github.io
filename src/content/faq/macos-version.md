---
question: Which macOS versions are supported?
category: Compatibility
order: 30
---

Aerial 4.1 requires **macOS 26 (Tahoe)** or later, and runs on macOS 27 (Golden Gate) as well. Apple Silicon and Intel Macs are both supported.

On **macOS 15 (Sequoia)**, use Aerial 4.0.16 from the [download page](/download#older). It's the last release of the 4.0 line, it won't be offered 4.1 as an update, and the Homebrew cask picks it for you automatically on Sequoia. For older macOS versions, Aerial 3 builds are still available but are no longer maintained.
