---
question: Can I add my own videos?
category: Library
order: 40
---

Yes. Open the Video Library (the third button in the popover's bottom bar) and look for **My Videos** in the sidebar: drop your videos there. Or copy your `.mov` and `.mp4` files into `/Users/Shared/Aerial/My Videos/` and Aerial picks them up on its own. Either way, you can then edit the title, location, scene and time of day from that same section.
