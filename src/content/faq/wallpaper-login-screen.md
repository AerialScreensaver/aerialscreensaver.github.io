---
question: Does the wallpaper really play on the lock screen?
category: Wallpaper
order: 63
---

Yes. Because Aerial is a native wallpaper extension, macOS keeps it as the backdrop of the lock screen when your Mac wakes or when you lock it, exactly like the built-in wallpapers. If you want your overlays there too, there's an option for that in Settings › Overlays.

The one place it can't play is the login screen right after a restart, before anyone has logged in: macOS shows a still image there instead.
