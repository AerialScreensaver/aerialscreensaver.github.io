---
question: Where do I get Aerial?
category: Getting started
order: 10
---

From the [download page](/download). Open the zip, drag `Aerial.app` into `/Applications` and open it from there. A short setup assistant asks how you want to use it, and after that everything is reachable from the Aerial icon in your menu bar (or in the Dock, if you chose that). Updates arrive on their own through Sparkle.
