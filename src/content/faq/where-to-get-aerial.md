---
question: Where do I get Aerial?
category: Getting started
order: 10
---

From the [download page](/download). Open the zip (Safari may automatically do it for you), drag `Aerial.app` into `/Applications` and open it from there. A short setup assistant asks how you want to use it,  then look for the Aerial icon in your menu bar (or in the dock, if you picked that mode). Updates arrive on their own through Sparkle, you can disable auto-updates in Settings if you prefer to install them manually.
