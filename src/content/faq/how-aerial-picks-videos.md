---
question: How does Aerial pick what to play?
category: Features
order: 56
---

The current selection drives the playlist. Every source, location, favourite, expansion and playlist in the Video Library has a **Play** button that makes it the selection, for every display or just one, and the filter row in the popover does the same with fewer clicks. The loop / shuffle / repeat-one button above the popover's playlist sets the order.

Time of day matters too. Most Apple videos are tagged sunrise, day, sunset or night, and Aerial uses your location (or the times you set in Settings › Time) to favour scenes that match the moment. There's also an option to play only night videos while your Mac is in Dark Mode.
