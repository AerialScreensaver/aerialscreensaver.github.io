---
question: If I quit the Aerial app, will the screensaver still work?
category: Getting started
order: 100
---

Yes. The screensaver and the wallpaper are a macOS extension bundled inside `Aerial.app`, and macOS runs it whether the app is open or not. Quitting the menu bar app stops neither of them.

What you do lose while the app is closed is everything it does on their behalf: pausing when windows cover the wallpaper (and the battery, thermal, Low Power Mode and camera pauses), Auto-Advance, global hotkeys, new video downloads, the data behind the weather and music overlays, and live feeds. So the usual setup is to leave it running in the menu bar. It's light.
