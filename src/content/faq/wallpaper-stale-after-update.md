---
question: The wallpaper looks stale or frozen after an update. What now?
category: Wallpaper
order: 68
---

After an update, macOS sometimes keeps the old copy of the wallpaper extension running. Aerial checks for that when it launches and restarts the wallpaper agent itself when needed, so this should be rare.

If it still happens, Settings › Wallpaper has a **Restart Wallpaper Agent** button under Troubleshooting, and an option to keep that button in the popover's bottom bar. Your desktop may briefly show a default wallpaper before Aerial comes back. Restarting the Mac does the same thing.
