---
question: "Multi-monitor: same or different videos per screen?"
category: Features
order: 54
---

Settings › Displays → **Viewing mode** has four options. **Independent** (the default) gives each display its own playlist and filter, and each pauses on its own. **Cloned** plays the same video on every display. **Mirrored** does the same but flips every other display, for a symmetrical look. **Spanned** stretches one video across all of them.

In Independent mode, you choose what a given display plays from its card on the Video Library's home screen, or with the **Play on** menu next to any Play button in the Library. For the screensaver you can also pick which displays play at all, and what happens when only the laptop screen is left.
