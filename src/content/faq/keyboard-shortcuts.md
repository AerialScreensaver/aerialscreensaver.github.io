---
question: Are there keyboard shortcuts?
category: Accessibility
order: 58
---

Yes, three system-wide ones: **toggle pause**, **previous video** and **next video**. They work no matter which app is in front, fullscreen ones included.

Turn them on in Settings › Accessibility → **Enable global shortcuts** (they're off by default). The defaults are ⌃⌥⌘ + Space, ⌃⌥⌘ + ← and ⌃⌥⌘ + →, deliberately unusual so they don't clash with anything else, and each one can be re-recorded or cleared on its own.
