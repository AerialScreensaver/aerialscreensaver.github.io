---
question: Why are some videos streamed instead of downloaded?
category: Library
order: 48
---

Videos in Aerial fall into two buckets. **Cacheable** videos (Apple's catalog and most expansion packs) download to disk and play from the cache: fast, smooth, and fine offline. **Live feeds** (YouTube live streams, cameras) play straight from the network, because they have no end and nothing to cache.

The popover marks live entries, and they need a connection while they're on screen. If a feed stalls, Aerial retries a few times and then moves on to a cached video rather than sitting on a blank frame.
