---
question: What's different about the wallpaper in the 4.1 beta?
category: Wallpaper
order: 61
badge: Beta
---

Aerial 4.1 replaces the wallpaper mode from 4.0 with a native macOS Wallpaper Extension. Aerial now shows up in System Settings › Wallpaper like a built-in wallpaper, and the video plays as your actual desktop — there is nothing underneath it. The easiest way to activate it is to accept the prompt Aerial shows at startup; you can also select Aerial manually in System Settings › Wallpaper.
