The same playback engine that drives the screensaver can run as your live desktop background. Wallpaper continuity keeps macOS's Mission Control and lock-screen wallpaper in sync with the playing video, so you never see a stale image when you wake from sleep. Aerial pauses automatically when most of the desktop is covered by windows — configurable threshold, battery-aware, and respects Night Shift.

### Native Wallpaper Extension <span class="rounded-full border border-aerial-accent/40 bg-aerial-accent/10 px-2 py-0.5 text-[11px] font-semibold uppercase tracking-wider text-aerial-accent">Beta</span>

In the 4.1 beta, Aerial is a native macOS Wallpaper Extension. It appears in System Settings › Wallpaper like a built-in wallpaper, and the video plays as your actual desktop — there's nothing underneath it. It even keeps playing on the login screen after your Mac wakes from sleep.

On first launch you pick how it behaves: **Still**, a frame from the current video that updates as scenes change — the way macOS wallpapers normally work — or **Live**, the video itself with window-aware auto-pause. Switch between the two anytime with the wallpaper Play/Pause button in the Aerial menu bar.

Auto-pause got smarter, too. Settings shows exactly which apps are covering the wallpaper, with live coverage percentages, and you can tell Aerial to ignore the ones that shouldn't count — like utilities that keep a transparent window over the whole screen. And if you'd rather never stare at the same scene all day, **Auto-Advance** moves to a new video on a schedule you pick, even while playback is paused.

*Requires macOS 26 (Tahoe) or later.*
