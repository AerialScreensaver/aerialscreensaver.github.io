Playing your own footage used to take several steps. Now you drop files into `/Users/Shared/Aerial/My Videos/`, or onto the My Videos section of the Video Library, and they show up next to Apple's. From there you can edit the title, location, scene and time of day, group them into playlists, or just leave them in the rotation. If a video comes out upside down because its orientation metadata is unusual, you can rotate it from the app.
