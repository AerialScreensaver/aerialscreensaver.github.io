Aerial 4 includes a visual overlay editor: pick a zone (a corner or center position), drag in a clock, date, location, weather, music, battery, timer, countdown or a message of your own, and watch it appear on a live preview. Fonts, colors, shadows, margins can all be tweaked to your taste.

Each display can have its own layout, and the wallpaper keeps a separate set from the screensaver, so you can run a busy layout on the screensaver and a quiet one behind your windows. The music overlay shows what's playing in any audio app, the same way the Now Playing widget does, and an option keeps overlays visible on the lock screen if you wish them to.
