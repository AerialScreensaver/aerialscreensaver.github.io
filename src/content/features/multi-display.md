Settings › Displays offers four viewing modes: **Independent**, a different video on each screen, each with its own filter; **Cloned**, the same video on every screen; **Mirrored**, the same video with every other screen flipped, for a symmetrical look; and **Spanned**, a single video stretched across all of them for one continuous shot, a long-time favourite of multi-monitor setups.

The screensaver gets its own display rules: pick which screens it plays on (the others go black), and you can override settings when external monitors are no longer connected.
