Beyond Apple's catalog, Aerial plays community-made packs from people who share their work with the project. Browse them in Video Library → Expansions, click install, and the new videos show up in your filters right away. Each pack has its own license, and nothing is installed until you ask for it. The [Expansions page](/expansions) shows what's available.
