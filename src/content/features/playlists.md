A playlist is your own list of videos from any source: Apple's catalog, expansions or your own files. Make as many as you like, set how long each video plays, export them as `.json` to carry them to another Mac, and switch to one from the popover whenever you want, say "background while I work" on one and "something nicer at the end of the day" on another.
