Three system-wide shortcuts: toggle pause, previous video and next video. Turn them on in Settings › Accessibility and record any combination you like. The defaults are ⌃⌥⌘ with Space, ← and →, chosen so they don't collide with anything else. Handy when a great shot comes up and you want to hold on it, or when one isn't the mood and you'd rather skip it without breaking your flow.
