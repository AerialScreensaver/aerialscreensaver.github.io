The 4.1 beta brings massive CPU-usage reductions in both the app and the extension, faster startup, and a seamless handoff between wallpaper and screensaver — no more flashes of stale frames when your Mac wakes or you log in.

The beta also picks better moments to stay quiet: optional pauses under thermal pressure, in Low Power Mode, and while your camera is in use — Aerial only reads the camera's on/off state, never the picture.
