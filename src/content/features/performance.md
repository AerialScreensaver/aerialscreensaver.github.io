Aerial 4.1 uses far less CPU than 4.0 did, in both the app and the wallpaper extension. It starts faster, and the handoff between wallpaper and screensaver no longer flashes a stale frame when your Mac wakes or you log in.

It also knows when to stay quiet. Beyond pausing when windows cover the desktop, the wallpaper can pause while your Mac runs on battery, under serious thermal pressure (on by default), in Low Power Mode, and while any app uses the camera. Aerial only reads whether the camera is on, never the picture.
