The Video Library opens on a home screen: a card for each display (a single card when they're cloned, mirrored or spanned) showing what's playing there, along with the viewing mode, playback speed, cache and overlays, and buttons to change any of it. It's also where Aerial tells you when it isn't set as your screensaver or wallpaper yet, with a one-click fix.

If you'd rather have Aerial in the Dock than in the menu bar, that's an option, and this window becomes its main window.
